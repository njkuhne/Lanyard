# dotlocal privacy policy

_Last updated: 26 October 2026_

dotlocal does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

- It has no analytics, tracking, advertising, or crash reporting.
- It makes no network connections of its own. When you click a service, macOS
  opens the address you entered in the app you'd expect (your browser, Finder,
  Screen Sharing, Terminal…), and that app handles the connection.
- Your server list lives in one file on your Mac, inside dotlocal's sandbox
  container. It never leaves your Mac unless you export it yourself.
- dotlocal never stores passwords. Sign-ins are handled by macOS and your
  Keychain.

Questions: open an issue at <https://github.com/njkuhne/dotlocal/issues>.
